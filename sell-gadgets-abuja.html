What does distress sale mean?

A distress sale is a fast sale where the seller needs cash quickly. Our distress-sale offers are typically 40–60% below market price. You trade price for speed, certainty and no haggling with strangers. If you want full market price and can wait, sell privately.

How fast do I get an offer?

What condition should my gadget be in?

For gadgets, working condition is what we want: screen, keyboard, battery and ports all functional. Old, faulty or condemned inverters, UPS units, solar equipment and transformers are also welcome.

Which inverter sizes do you prefer?

We prefer 5kVA, 10kVA, 15kVA, 20kVA and 30kVA units.
