Generator Repair problems we fix in Abuja

  • Generator won't start or cranks and dies
  • Surging, hunting or unstable output
  • Black, white or blue smoke from the exhaust
  • Loss of power under load or tripping the breaker
  • Overheating, coolant or oil leaks
  • Fuel system faults: blocked filters, air in the diesel line, dirty carburettor
  • Faulty AVR, alternator or control panel
  • Auto-changeover and ATS problems on standby sets

How a FixIt call-out works

  1. Tell us the make, size (kVA) and what the generator is doing, by form or WhatsApp.
  2. We confirm a quote before a technician is dispatched.
  3. The technician diagnoses on site, repairs with common spares carried or sourced for your model, and tests under load.
⚠ Generator safety

Never run a generator indoors or in an enclosed space. Carbon monoxide is invisible and can be fatal within minutes.
