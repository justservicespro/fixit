How a FixIt call-out works

  1. Tell us the brand, model and what is wrong.
  2. We quote before dispatch; common spares such as chargers and RAM are carried.
  3. The technician repairs on site where possible, or advises if the device needs workshop repair.
⚠ Swollen battery

If a laptop battery is swollen or the case bulges, stop using and charging it. Do not puncture it; get a technician to remove it safely.

Protect your data

Back up your files before any repair if you can, and tell the technician which files matter most. A failing drive should be switched off and not used until it has been checked.
