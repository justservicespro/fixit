  2. We give you a quote before a plumber is dispatched.
  3. The plumber isolates the water, repairs the fault and tests for leaks.
⚠ Water and electricity

If water has reached sockets or the electrical panel, switch off power at the main breaker from a dry position before doing anything else.

Stop the damage first

If water is escaping, switch off the water at the main valve or pump while you wait. Keep electrical items away from wet areas and switch off power if water is near sockets.
