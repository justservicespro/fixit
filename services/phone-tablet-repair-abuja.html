How a FixIt call-out works

  1. Tell us the model and the problem.
  2. We quote before dispatch, including whether original or compatible parts will be used.
  3. The technician repairs and tests the device with you.
⚠ Swollen battery

A swollen phone battery is a fire risk. Stop charging it, keep it away from heat and have it removed by a technician.

Before you hand over your phone

Back up your data if you can and note your passcode needs with the technician. Ask for the old part back, especially a replaced battery. If a battery is swollen, stop using the phone.
