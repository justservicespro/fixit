Safety tips when hiring directly

  • Agree the scope and price in writing (WhatsApp is fine) before any work starts.
  • Pay for parts against a receipt where possible, and avoid large upfront payments.
  • Ask for ID, check reviews or past work, and be present for work involving gas, electrics or heights.
  • Listed artisans are independent. FixIt Abuja introduces you but does not employ or supervise them. For vetted dispatch with our backing, use Book a Service.
  • Something went wrong? Report a listing on WhatsApp.
