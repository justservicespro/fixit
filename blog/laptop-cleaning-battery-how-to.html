How to Clean Your Laptop and Protect Its Battery — Safely

By FixIt Abuja·15 September 2026·3 min read

Most laptop damage from 'cleaning' comes from using the wrong materials, not from cleaning itself. A careful routine keeps a laptop looking and working better for years.

Exterior and keyboard cleaning

  1. Shut the laptop down completely and unplug it before cleaning.
  2. Use a microfibre cloth lightly dampened with water, or an approved screen cleaner, on the display — never spray liquid directly onto it.
  3. Turn the laptop upside down and gently shake or use compressed air to dislodge crumbs and dust from between keys.
  4. Wipe the keyboard and casing with a barely damp cloth, avoiding any liquid pooling near ports or the keyboard's edges.
  5. Let everything air dry fully before powering back on.

✓ Do

  • Keep the battery between roughly 20% and 80% charge for daily use where possible.
  • Let it fully discharge and recharge occasionally if the software allows battery calibration.
  • Avoid leaving the laptop plugged in at 100% for extended periods, especially in heat.
  • Store an unused laptop at around 50% charge, not fully charged or fully flat.

✕ Don't

  • Don't leave the laptop in direct sun or a hot car — heat degrades battery capacity faster than almost anything else.
  • Don't use it on a soft surface while charging, which combines heat build-up with blocked airflow.
  • Don't ignore a battery that's visibly swollen or a laptop that no longer sits flat.
  • Don't use third-party chargers of uncertain quality — voltage mismatches can damage the battery over time.
⚠ Safety

A swollen laptop battery is a genuine hazard — stop using the laptop, don't attempt to remove the battery yourself unless you're experienced, and take it to a technician for safe disposal and replacement.

Tip: If your laptop is mostly used plugged in at a desk, check whether the manufacturer offers a 'battery care' or charge-limit mode in its settings — many now let you cap charging at 80% specifically to extend long-term battery life.

Key takeaways

  • Never spray liquid directly onto a screen or keyboard — dampen the cloth instead.
  • Keeping charge between 20–80% extends battery lifespan meaningfully.
  • Heat is the single biggest threat to battery health.
  • A swollen battery needs professional handling, not a DIY fix.
